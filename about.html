About Firm Ground Homes

Firm Ground Homes is a nonprofit that runs shared transitional homes for adults rebuilding after homelessness, job loss, reentry or housing instability.

Our story

Sample story

Firm Ground Homes began with a simple observation: many people who work, pay what they can and follow the rules still cannot get into a traditional apartment. Credit history, rental history, past convictions and rising deposits stand in the way.

So we opened a shared home with a different set of terms: a clean, safe room, clear rules and fair rent. Today we run three homes with about 3 shared rooms each.

A real client tells this story in their own words.

Our mission

To give working adults a stable, structured place to live while they get back on their feet.

Values

What we believe

Dignity

Every resident is a capable adult. We speak plainly, protect privacy and leave pity out of it.

Clear standards

Second chance, real standards. Rules are written down and the same for everyone.

Honesty

We state costs, screening and expectations up front. No surprises.

Stepping stones

A stay here is a step, not a destination. We plan the next move with each resident from the first week.

House standards

The same rules for everyone

Every resident agrees to the same house rules. We share the full house agreement at your interview, and we ask every resident to follow it.

  • Pay your rent
  • Respect the home
  • Respect each other
