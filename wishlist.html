What we can accept

New or gently used, clean and ready to use:

  • Bedding and towels
  • Cleaning supplies and laundry detergent
  • Pots, pans, dishes and utensils
  • Work clothing in good condition
  • Unopened toiletries
  • Transit passes and gift cards

What we cannot accept

We have limited storage in shared homes, so please do not bring:

  • Mattresses, large furniture or appliances
  • Anything that needs repair
  • Opened toiletries or food
  • Clothing that is not clean or in good condition

Drop-off

Drop-offs are by appointment. Call (555) 010-0142 or email hello@firmgroundhomes.example, weekdays, 9 am to 5 pm. (Sample contact details.) We never share the address of a home. We give you a drop-off spot when you book.
