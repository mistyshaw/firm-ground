Questions and contact

Straight answers to the questions we hear most. If yours is not here, call or email us.

Questions

Who can live at Firm Ground Homes?
Adults 18 or older who have income or are actively looking for work, and who are ready to live in a shared room in a shared home with house rules.
How much does it cost?
$125 to $175 per week on a sliding scale based on income. Utilities and Wi-Fi are included. There is a refundable deposit equal to one week's rent, and no hidden fees. (Sample figures.)
How long can I stay?
There is no set length of stay. This is a stepping stone, not a permanent home, so we start planning your next move with you from the first week.
Will I have my own room?
No. Rooms are shared, and each home has about 3 rooms. You also share the kitchen, living room and bathrooms.
Do you run background checks?
We review every application one at a time, and a background review is part of screening. A past conviction, eviction or low credit score does not automatically rule you out.
What do I need to bring to my interview?
Photo ID, proof of income or a list of your job search steps, and one emergency contact. A referral letter helps if someone referred you. If you are missing something, apply anyway and we will help you gather it. How to apply
How fast will I hear back?
A staff member calls within 3 business days, and we give a decision within 5 business days.
Do you offer counseling or medical care?
No. We are not a medical or clinical facility. If you need urgent help, call or text 988, or dial 211.
Is my information kept private?
Yes. We never share your information. We ask only for what we need, and the application form does not ask about your background. We talk about that with you by phone.
Can I refer someone?
Yes. Case managers, courts, VA offices, hospitals and other agencies can send a referral. The person must know about it and agree to be contacted. For referral partners
Can I visit a home?
We do not publish the address of any home, to protect residents' privacy. A tour is part of the interview, and we share the location then.
