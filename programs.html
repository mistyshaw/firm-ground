Programs

Programs and eligibility

Everything you need to decide if Firm Ground Homes is right for you, including what it costs and what we expect. No surprises.

Housing

A bed in a shared room, in a home with about 3 rooms. You share the kitchen, living room and bathrooms. Utilities and Wi-Fi are included in your weekly payment.

There is no set length of stay. This is a stepping stone, not a permanent home, so we start planning your next move with you from the first week.

Job and education help

  • Resume and interview coaching
  • Help finding GED, certificate and training programs
  • Introductions to local employers
  • Bus passes and work clothing when funds allow

The plain details

What it costs, what we expect, what we screen for

What it costs

  • $125 to $175 per week, set on a sliding scale based on income
  • Utilities and Wi-Fi included
  • A refundable deposit equal to one week's rent
  • No hidden fees

What we expect

  • Pay your rent on time
  • Follow the house agreement
  • Respect the home and the people in it
  • Meet with staff once a month to review your goals

What we screen for

  • Income, or an active job search
  • A background review, done case by case
  • A signed house agreement

Concept note: Client to confirm fees, screening practices and eligibility criteria, with their board or counsel.

Fit

Is this the right fit?

This is a good fit if you are 18 or older, you have income or are looking for work, and you are ready to live in a shared home with rules. It is shared living in a shared room, not a private apartment, and it is not a medical facility. If you need urgent care, call or text 988, or dial 211.

A past conviction, eviction or low credit score does not automatically rule you out. We look at every application one at a time.
