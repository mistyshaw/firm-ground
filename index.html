Why we exist

Housing can be out of reach even when you work.

Credit history, rental history, past convictions and rising deposits keep many capable people on the outside. Firm Ground Homes is a different option: a room in a clean, safe shared home with clear rules, fair rent and support that helps you move forward.

It is a stepping stone, not a permanent address. You stay as long as you need to get steady, and we help you plan what comes next.

Three steps

How it works

1

Programs

What we offer

A place in a shared home

A bed in a shared room, shared kitchen and living areas, and utilities included in one weekly payment.

Job and education help

Resume support, interview practice, GED and training referrals, and employer connections.

Our standards

Second chance, real standards.

Every resident agrees to the same house rules: pay your rent, respect the home, respect each other. We screen applicants, and we expect everyone to hold up their end.
